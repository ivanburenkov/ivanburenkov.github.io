Research Highlights

Quantum Error Correction & Architecture

Developing error-tailored architectures using single-shot quantum measurement accuracy estimates. Recently demonstrated a record 40 dB improvement in error correction efficiency below classical limits.

Quantum Sensing & Biophotonics

Led the development of a single-photon sensitive Quantum Flow Cytometer for rapid biomarker detection. This project was awarded as a Stage 1 Winner in the NIH Quantum Sensing Technology Challenge.

Metropolitan Quantum Networks

Pioneering the implementation of the DCQ-net across the Washington D.C. area, achieving entanglement distribution over deployed 100+ km fiber links utilizing advanced phase-stabilization techniques.

Super-Resolution Imaging (S-RIPE)

Invented Super-Resolution Imaging via Photon Enumeration. Leveraging photon-number-resolving measurements to bypass classical diffraction limits for highly sensitive imaging applications.

Interactive Web Tools

Bridging hardware and software, I develop browser-based applications to advance open science in quantum optics. Leveraging C, C++, and Python via WebAssembly and Pyodide to bring intensive computational tools directly to researchers.
